Dog Nutrition & Safety

Toxic Foods (Never Feed)

  • Chocolate: Contains theobromine which is toxic to a dog's heart and nervous system.
  • Onions & Garlic: Can cause gastrointestinal irritation and red blood cell damage.
  • Grapes & Raisins: Extremely dangerous as they can cause sudden kidney failure.

Healthy Diet

  • Balanced Kibble: High-quality commercial dog food formulated for their age and size.
  • Safe Vegetables: Cooked carrots, pumpkin, and green beans are excellent low-calorie snacks.
  • Hydration: Always ensure a bowl of fresh, clean water is available 24/7.

Essential Health Tips

Vaccination

Keep up with annual shots, especially for Rabies and Parvovirus.

Deworming

Regular treatment for internal parasites is vital for their weight and energy.

Grooming

Brush their coat daily and bathe them once a month to prevent skin issues.

Daily Exercise

Walking your dog daily prevents obesity and reduces anxiety.

Health is Priority #1

Don't wait for your dog to look sick. Regular check-ups with a veterinarian can extend their life by years.
